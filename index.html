Post your own short clips on schedule

A personal tool that runs on the creator's own computer.

ClipBot Scheduler helps a creator turn long videos they have permission to use into short vertical clips with captions, and upload those clips to their own TikTok account at times they choose.

  • Runs locally on the creator's own Mac. There is no ClipBot server or user database.
  • Connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, after the creator logs in and approves.
  • Uploads clips to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts, or posts them, only when the creator has set this up.
  • The creator reviews every clip before it is published.
