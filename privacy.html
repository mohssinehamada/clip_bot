Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

In short: ClipBot Scheduler runs on your own computer. We do not operate servers that collect, store or sell your data. Your TikTok login stays on your device and is used only to upload the clips you choose.

1. Information the App accesses

  • TikTok account information: when you connect TikTok, the App receives an access token, a refresh token, your TikTok account ID (open_id) and the permissions you granted (basic profile information and video upload or publishing). It uses these only to upload clips to your account and to check their upload status.
  • Videos and captions: the source videos you add, the clips the App creates, and the captions and hashtags for them.
  • YouTube information: public video information (titles, channel names, view counts, upload dates and license type) from YouTube API Services, used to suggest videos you may be allowed to clip.

2. Where your information is stored

All of this information is stored only on your own computer. TikTok tokens are kept in a private folder that only your computer user account can read. We do not have access to it.

3. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The App sends information only to these services, and only to do what you asked:

  • TikTok: the clips you choose to upload, and their captions when you use direct posting. See the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube API Services (Google): search requests for public videos. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): the text transcript of a source video, so it can suggest the best moments for clips. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Speech-to-text transcription happens on your computer. Your audio is not uploaded for transcription.

4. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your computer until you delete it. To remove the App's access to TikTok, disconnect it in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the App's stored login files. Deleting the App's folder removes all clips, transcripts and settings it created.

5. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process information about children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
